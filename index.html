Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no subscription, no premium tier and no ads. It's a hobby project, made for tired parents.

What do I need?

Two Android phones (or a phone and a tablet) running Android 8.0 or newer. Wi-Fi Direct mode needs Android 10 or newer on both. An old phone makes a perfect baby camera; keep it plugged in overnight.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Not yet! For now you install it directly from this page, which takes about a minute.

How do I install it?
  1. On each phone, open this page and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning about an unrecognised app, choose Install anyway.
Does it drain the battery?

Streaming video uses power, so keep the baby phone on its charger overnight. The parent phone shows the baby phone's battery level, so you'll know if it's running low.

Can I watch from a tablet?

Yes. BabyCam has a layout for tablets and foldables, in portrait and landscape.

Does anyone else see my video?

No. The stream goes straight between your two phones, encrypted, and is never uploaded or recorded. Nobody, including us, can watch it.
